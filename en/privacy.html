Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Controller

AIVa UG (haftungsbeschränkt), Dieselstraße 5, 70839 Gerlingen, Germany, email bianca@enderlin.info. We have not appointed a data protection officer as there is no legal obligation to do so. As our company is based in the EU, this policy follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for all users, including users outside the EU.

2. In short

  • Your chat history and profile are stored on your device (browser storage). We do not store your chat messages on our servers.
  • So a coach can answer, we send your latest messages and – for Markus – your profile to our AI provider Anthropic (USA).
  • For the free trial we store your email address. Payments are handled by Stripe.
  • We use no advertising or analytics cookies and no tracking. We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

3. Hosting

The app is hosted by Netlify, Inc. (San Francisco, USA). Netlify processes technically necessary data such as IP address, time, page requested and browser type to deliver the app and protect it against abuse (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers to the USA are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. To prevent abuse we also count chat messages per IP address per day; this counter is kept in memory only and discarded by the next day at the latest.

4. Storage on your device

The app stores in your browser (localStorage): chosen language, email address, trial start, access status, your profile (age, height, weight, location, intolerances), the partner link you arrived with, and recent chat messages per coach. This is strictly necessary for the app to work. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

5. Email address and trial

When you start the trial, we store your email address in our customer database (Airtable, Formagrid Inc., San Francisco, USA) to link your access and contact you about your trial and subscription (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). If you do not subscribe, we delete it no later than 12 months after the trial ends; otherwise after the end of the contract, unless statutory retention obligations apply. If you request the Whoop setup, we also store that request so we can get back to you.

6. Chatting with the coaches (AI)

When you message a coach, we send your latest messages (up to 12), your chosen language and – for Markus only – your profile to Anthropic PBC (San Francisco, USA), which generates the answer. Under its commercial terms Anthropic does not use data from its API to train its models. Our servers do not store your messages; we only log technical metrics (amount of text processed, coach chosen). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Health data: Details such as weight, intolerances, allergies, sleep, injuries or how you feel are health data. We process them only with your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give when creating your profile. You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future – by email or by deleting your profile and chat history (clear site data). Only share in the chat what you want to share.

7. Payment

Subscriptions are paid via Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. (Dublin, Ireland). You enter your payment details directly with Stripe; we do not receive them. Stripe gives us your name, email address, plan, payment status and – if you came via a partner link – the partner's name, so we can pay the partner's commission. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR (retention of accounting records for up to 10 years under German law). More: stripe.com/privacy.

8. Cancellation and withdrawal

If you cancel or withdraw using the buttons in the app, we process the form data (type of notice, name, email, plan, date, reason if any) and the date and time of receipt. The data passes through our automation service n8n (n8n GmbH, Berlin) and is stored there; we send the confirmation from our Google (Gmail) mailbox. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and to object (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3) GDPR). Just email bianca@enderlin.info. These rights also cover the rights to know, delete and correct under US state privacy laws such as California's. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, e.g. the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Baden-Württemberg.

10. Children

The app is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
